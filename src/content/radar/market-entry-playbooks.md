Every country genuinely is different. The same questions still recur every single time: what
the licence permits, whether the partner is ready, how good the rail is, what the settlement
risk is, what the customer must be told, what the support load will be.

Carrying that in your head makes launch two slower than it should be and makes launch five
impossible to compare with launch three. I learned this the expensive way.
