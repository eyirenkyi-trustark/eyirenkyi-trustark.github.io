The moment velocity leaves the team it stops measuring delivery and starts measuring
estimation behaviour. Points inflate, scope fragments, and nobody lies about it exactly, they
just gradually stop telling you anything.

Report cycle time, or report outcomes. Leave velocity with the team that generates it.
