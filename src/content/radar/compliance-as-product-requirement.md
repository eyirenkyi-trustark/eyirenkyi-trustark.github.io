Compliance handled as a launch gate produces a corridor, a service or a feature that was
compliant once. Handled as a product requirement it stays compliant while it grows, because
the controls are in the system rather than in somebody's memory.

The test is simple. If a control only works when a person remembers to apply it, it does not
work.
