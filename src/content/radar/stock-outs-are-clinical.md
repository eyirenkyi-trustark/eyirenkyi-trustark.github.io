---
title: 'Stock-outs are a clinical metric'
ring: adopt
quadrant: 'Product practice'
blurb: 'In health supply chains, measure availability at the counter, not fill rate at the warehouse. The patient does not experience your inventory system.'
updated: 2026-09-12
moved: new
---

A warehouse can report a healthy fill rate while a patient two hundred kilometres away is sent
home without medicine. Those are not the same number and only one of them is the outcome.

Pick the metric the person at the counter would recognise. If your reporting improves while
their experience does not, you are measuring the wrong end of the chain.
