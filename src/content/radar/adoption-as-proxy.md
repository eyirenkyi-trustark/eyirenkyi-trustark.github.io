Adoption is the easiest platform metric to move and the easiest to move dishonestly. Mandate
the tool and adoption goes up. Remove the alternative and adoption goes up.

Hold does not mean do not measure it. It means do not let it stand alone. The number that
matters sits next to it: time to first success, steps removed, or how long someone waits when
something breaks.
